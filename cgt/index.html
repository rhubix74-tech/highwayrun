Highway Run · Quick Start

Your profile is ready. Here's what you need to know before your first run.

1 · Speed = points

Drive fast and pass through the open gaps in traffic. Your speed increases the points earned from successful passes. Keep a clean streak going to build your multiplier.

2 · Miles & distance

The MILES counter shows how far you've travelled during this run. Your run starts with 3:00 on the clock. Earn time bonuses by reaching each 100,000-point milestone.

3 · Exits & cash

An exit appears every 10 miles (every 5 in the Zonda R). Move to the far-right exit lane and take the ramp to end your run early and bank your points as cash. Every 100,000 banked points is worth $5.

4 · The objective

Build the highest score you can, travel as far as possible, and choose when to cash out. If the timer reaches zero or you press Esc, the run ends and unbanked points are forfeited.

5 · Traffic & crashes

Thread through gaps and weave between lanes to build WEAVE combos. Hitting traffic breaks your streak and costs time; hitting the shoulder or barrier also slows you down. Brake when all lanes are blocked.

6 · Controls

W / ↑ accelerate · S / ↓ brake · A / D or ← / → steer · Space handbrake · E activate equipped Nitrous or Ghost Chassis · C change camera · Esc abandon the run.

7 · Power-ups

Buy single-use power-ups in the Shop and equip them before a run. Insurance protects against the first crash's time and point penalties; Nitrous gives a 5-second boost; x2 Points doubles point multipliers; Ghost Chassis gives 30 seconds of invulnerability. Ghost Chassis and Nitrous cannot be equipped together. The RPG Power-up, unlocked from Scout’s surprise, replaces all other power-ups and fires a traffic-clearing rocket with a 90-second reload.

8 · Your car matters

There will be many cars with different driving styles and maybe even bonuses
